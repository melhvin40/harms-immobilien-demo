3. No third-party services in the page build

This website loads no fonts, maps, videos, scripts or icons from third-party servers. All font files are delivered from our own server. Simply opening the page therefore creates no connection to servers beyond the processing described here.

4. Cookies, consent and audience measurement

No cookies are set for analytics or advertising. Analytics and marketing functions are technically prepared but switched off by default and are not executed without your express consent.

If you give or refuse consent, your decision is stored locally in your browser so that you are not asked again on every visit. This storage does not take place on our server.

5. Contact form and enquiries by email

If you use the enquiry form we process your name, your email address, optionally your telephone number, the selected nature of your enquiry and your message. We use these details solely to handle your enquiry.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry is directed at concluding or performing a contract, and otherwise Article 6(1)(a) GDPR on the basis of your consent. You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

6. Calculation aids on this website

The capitalised value calculator and the ground rent check run entirely in your browser. The values you enter are not transmitted to our server, not stored and not analysed. They leave your device only if you carry them over into the enquiry form yourself.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Article 15 GDPR), rectification (Article 16), erasure (Article 17), restriction of processing (Article 18), data portability (Article 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21). Please use the contact details above.

Independently of this you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. In North Rhine-Westphalia the competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia.
