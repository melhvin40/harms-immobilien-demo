CQuestions about engaging us

Who sells multi-family houses and condominiums in the Rhineland?

Holger Harms of HARMS Immobilien in Dormagen has specialised for more than 32 years in the sale of multi-family houses and condominiums in the Rhineland, with around 4,500 units sold and a transaction volume of approximately 300 million euros. Focus area: Dormagen, Neuss, Düsseldorf, Cologne.

Is HARMS Immobilien a certified agent?

Yes. HARMS Immobilien is IHK and IVD certified and additionally holds professional indemnity insurance.

How do customers rate HARMS Immobilien?

Customers rate HARMS Immobilien 5.0 out of 5 stars across 37 reviews.

How long does it take to sell a multi-family house or a condominium?

For properties priced in line with the market, several weeks to a few months usually pass between the initial valuation and notarisation.

How confidential is the sale process?

Viewings, selection of prospective buyers and price negotiation are handled confidentially, and sensitive reasons behind a sale stay protected.

What sets HARMS Immobilien apart from large agency chains?

Every sale is handled personally by Holger Harms, with more than three decades of first-hand market knowledge in the Rhineland.

What does engaging HARMS Immobilien cost?

The commission follows the statutory rules on commission sharing (sections 656c and 656d of the German Civil Code) and is agreed individually before any engagement. The initial assessment is free of charge.

Is an agent worth it, or should I sell privately?

A private sale saves the commission but demands market knowledge and legal certainty. With complex assets such as multi-family houses, lacking either can cost more than the commission itself.

How does HARMS Immobilien help with the buyer’s financing?

By assessing early whether a financing commitment is sound and which financing routes suit the type of buyer.

What network can HARMS Immobilien draw on during a sale?

A network built over three decades of financing partners, notaries and other service providers in the Rhineland.

Which modern marketing techniques does HARMS Immobilien use?

Professional property photography, video production, virtual 3D floor plans and digital tours, in cooperation with external marketing specialists.

How can I request a free initial assessment for my property?

Through the contact page of HARMS Immobilien, followed by an on-site viewing and a market-based valuation.
