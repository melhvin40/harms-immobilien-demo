03The three transaction pillars
  1. 1

    Apartment buildings and multi-family houses

    Sale, acquisition and structured valuation of existing properties, judged on capitalised value, tenant structure and state of repair rather than on portal reach.

  2. 2

    Heritable building rights and special situations

    Valuation under ImmoWertV, ground rent adjustment under section 9a ErbbauRG, negotiation with foundations and municipalities as landowners, a field that standard agencies regularly underestimate.

  3. 3

    Confidential off-market placement

    Targeted approach to vetted family offices and high net worth private investors, without a property ever becoming publicly visible.

05Questions about the company

Which cities and districts does HARMS Immobilien cover?

HARMS Immobilien works in the Rhineland. The area covered comprises the cities of Düsseldorf, Cologne, Dormagen and Neuss as well as the districts of Mettmann and Rhein-Kreis Neuss. The registered office is in Dormagen, Latoursgarten 11.

Which property types does HARMS Immobilien handle?

Apartment buildings and multi-family houses, residential and commercial properties, and heritable building rights. It also covers special situations such as non-performing portfolios and communities of heirs.

Who does HARMS Immobilien work for?

For owners of investment property, for family offices and high net worth private investors, and for credit institutions, insolvency administrators and communities of heirs.

What does off-market mean at HARMS Immobilien?

Properties are not listed publicly. Buyers are approached selectively and are vetted beforehand, so that neither tenants nor the owner’s professional circle learn of the sale before it is concluded.

What are Holger Harms’s qualifications?

Holger Harms is an IHK certified real estate and housing merchant and a Real Estate Management Expert (CCI/IHK). He has run HARMS Immobilien since 2007 and has more than 32 years of market experience in real estate and housing.
